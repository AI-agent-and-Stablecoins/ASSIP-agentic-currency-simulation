Agentic AI and Preferences for Medium of Exchange

Let’s start the paper with a distinction of the different features of a stablecoin:

- Governance features (what is the asset backed by)  
- What does it track \- USD or Euro etc  
- Liquidity on an exchange (bid-ask spread)  
- Volatility of the stablecoin \- how close does it stay to a dollar  
- What exchange it trades on: gas fees associated with it


(1 or 0 Genius Act compliant, liquidity, ability to trade near the 1 dollar mark, gas fees, Euro v USD)

Start our discussion of the purpose of a stablecoin \- hold value over time and act as a medium of exchange  
 

Hypotheses:

1) The more risk averse the AI agent, the more the Agent will favor USD over EURO.  
2) The more risk averse the AI agent, the more the Agent will favor liquidity (low bid-ask spread) over gas fee concerns.  
3) The more risk averse the AI agent, the more the Agent will favor proper governance (Genius Act Compliance) over liquidity.  
4) The closer a banking crisis (or depegging event) is in the mind of an AI agent, the more an AI agent will prefer to use a gold backed stablecoin.  
5) In cross border transactions, AI agents will prefer USD stablecoins over Euro stablecoins the more the relative volatility of the two currencies
